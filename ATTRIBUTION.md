# Attribution

## Original showcase materials

The illustrations in the public/places directory, map artwork in the
public/gallery directory, product interface, profile mockup, social preview
and synthetic fixtures were created specifically for this showcase by
Iker Pérez. No external image, font, map tile, map dataset or player profile
is used.

The map illustration is a hand-drawn, simplified visual motif. It does not
claim geographic accuracy and is not based on OpenStreetMap or another
mapping provider.

## Third-party materials

No third-party image or map assets are currently included. Software
dependencies keep their original licenses; see the package metadata and
their upstream notices.

Any future external asset must be reviewed and recorded here with its
creator, source, license and required attribution before it is added.
